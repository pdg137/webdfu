STM32C071 WebUSB DFU

Firmware Download (write to USB device)

Firmware Upload (read from USB device)

About

This page is for programming an STM32C071 over its built-in USB DFU bootloader.

Prerequisites

  • A recent Chromium-based browser with WebUSB support.
  • An STM32C071 connected in DFU bootloader mode over USB.
  • A firmware image ready to upload.
